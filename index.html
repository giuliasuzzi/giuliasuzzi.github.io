About

A connected practice.

Medicine provides the empirical ground; theory and cultural analysis provide other ways of asking what those findings mean.

I am a medical student and researcher at the University of Florence, with an interdisciplinary background spanning medicine, psychology, neuroscience and earlier studies in mathematics.

Alongside scientific research, I pursue independent work in philosophy and critical theory, as well as literary and cultural analysis.
