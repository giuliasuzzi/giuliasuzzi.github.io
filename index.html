I am a medical student and interdisciplinary researcher working across medicine, psychiatry, ophthalmology, neuroscience, philosophy and literary-cultural analysis. My scientific work examines neuropsychiatric disease, self-harm and developmental and relational processes; my theoretical and cultural work explores identity, agency, embodiment and the limits of classification.

About

A connected practice.

Scientific research, theoretical inquiry and cultural reading are different methods for attending to related questions.

I am a final-year medical student and researcher at the University of Florence, with an interdisciplinary background spanning medicine, psychology, neuroscience and earlier studies in mathematics.

Alongside scientific research, I pursue independent work in philosophy and critical theory, as well as literary and cultural analysis.
