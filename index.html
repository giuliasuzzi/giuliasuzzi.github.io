Why the retina?
The retina provides an accessible neural tissue in which structure, microvasculature, and electrophysiological function can be studied non-invasively. The NRBF project examines whether this accessibility can be used to organize evidence concerning neuropsychiatric and neurodegenerative disease.
What is oculomics?
Oculomics refers broadly to the use of ocular measurements as potential biomarkers of systemic, neurological, or neurodegenerative processes. The approach is particularly interesting because modern retinal imaging can provide quantitative measurements in vivo.
What makes a biomarker useful?
A measurable difference is not automatically a clinically useful biomarker. Questions of specificity, reproducibility, longitudinal validity, confounding, standardization, and clinical utility remain central to interpreting retinal biomarkers.
